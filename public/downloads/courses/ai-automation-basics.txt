Lesson: AI Automation Basics for Small Businesses

What to do first:
- List repetitive tasks your team does every day.
- Mark the tasks that affect leads, follow-up, or revenue.
- Choose one workflow for a first automation pilot.

Checklist:
- Define the trigger.
- Define the required input fields.
- Define the output or handoff step.
- Test with one real team member before rollout.
